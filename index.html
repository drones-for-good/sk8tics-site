What it does

Log a session in the time it takes to lace up

Four marks per element — landed, missed, fell, not attempted. No typing on cold hands. The week’s pattern is the point, not the individual tick.

Score a run-through against the real scale of values

Base values come from the current ISU communication, not an estimate, for singles, ice dance and pairs. A coach picks a GOE per element and the total is the total a panel would reach.

Import a competition instead of typing it

Find the skate on Skate Ontario or skatingresults.ca and the whole protocol comes across: base values, every judge’s mark, the program components with their factors, and the deduction with its reason. Then the coach adds what the sheet left out.

Compare two competitions, including the gap to first

Pick any two skates and see what moved — element by element, and against the winner’s sheet from the same segment.

Who it’s for

Skaters

Your own record, including every note a coach has written against an element. Yours to read, and yours to delete.

Coaches

Score a run-through, record a competition, and keep a season’s worth of context on each skater you work with. A skater has to accept the link, and can end it whenever they like.

What we don’t do

No advertising. No analytics. No third-party trackers of any kind — there is not one in the app, which is unusual enough to be worth saying plainly. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone for marketing.

A coach sees your log because you let them. That permission is enforced in the database itself, not just in the app, and it ends the moment you revoke it. How we handle data.
