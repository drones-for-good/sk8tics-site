Account deletion

Delete your Sk8tics account

Two ways to do it. Both remove the same things, and neither needs you to ask anyone’s permission.

From inside the app

Open Sk8tics, tap your initials in the top corner to open the account sheet, and scroll to Delete account. You will be asked to type your username to confirm.

It happens immediately and cannot be undone. We keep no copy, and there is no grace period to change your mind.

By email, without the app

Write to ying.skating@gmail.com from the email address on the account, with the subject Delete my Sk8tics account. Include your username if you remember it. We will delete it and reply to confirm.

A parent or guardian may make this request for a child’s account. Say so in the message and give the child’s username; we do not need the child’s agreement.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in and your email address.
  • Your profile — username, name, discipline.
  • Every practice session and every element mark in it.
  • Your programmes and their planned content.
  • Every run-through score a coach recorded for you, and their notes.
  • Every competition record saved against you.
  • Your links to coaches.
  • Any device registered for notifications.

If you are a coach, the records you wrote about other skaters are deleted too, since they are part of your account.

What is not affected

Competition protocols published by Skate Ontario and skatingresults.ca are their documents on their websites. Deleting a Sk8tics account removes our copy of a result; it has no bearing on the official record, which we do not control and cannot change.

How long it takes

RouteWhen it is gone
In the appImmediately, before the screen closes.
By emailWithin 30 days, and normally within a few days.

Nothing is retained after deletion for analytics or any other purpose, because there is no analytics. Routine encrypted infrastructure backups may hold a copy for a short period before rotating out; they are not readable by the app and are never used to restore an individual account.
