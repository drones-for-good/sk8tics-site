Privacy policy

What we keep, and what we don’t.

Effective 27 September 2026 · Sk8tics, Ontario, Canada

Sk8tics is a practice log for figure skaters and their coaches. This page describes every category of information the app holds, who can reach it, and how to remove it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version

There is no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking in this app. Nothing is sold. Nothing is shared for marketing. The only people who can see a skater’s log are that skater and a coach they have personally linked with.

What the app collects

CategoryWhat, exactlyWhy
Account Email address, a username you choose, your name, whether you are under 18, whether you are a skater or a coach, and which discipline you skate. To sign you in and to show the right scale of values.
Your training Practice sessions, the marks against each element, your programmes and their planned content, and any notes. It is the log. This is the app.
Coach scoring Run-through scores a linked coach records for you, element by element, with their notes. So you can read what your coach saw.
Competition records Results imported from officially published protocols: competitor name, club, placement, element and component scores, and the individual judges’ marks. To keep a competition record without retyping a sheet.
Notifications A device token, if you allow notifications, and your two on/off preferences. To tell you when a coach has scored you.

A note on competition results

Competition protocols are documents Skate Ontario and skatingresults.ca publish openly on the web. Importing one copies information that is already public into your own record. Because a protocol lists everyone in a segment, a coach can also save another skater’s result to compare against — exactly as they could by keeping the printed sheet.

What the app does not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising.
  • No analytics or usage tracking. There is no analytics SDK in the app.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.
  • No payment information. The app is free and has nothing to buy.
  • No purchase of data about you from anybody else.

Who can see your information

  • You — all of it.
  • A coach you have linked with — your log, programmes, scores and competition records. A link needs both sides to agree, and either side can end it. When it ends, the coach loses access immediately.
  • Nobody else. Not other skaters, not other coaches, not your club.

These rules are enforced by the database, through row-level security, and not only by the app. A request for someone else’s data returns nothing even if it is made outside the app entirely. That property is checked by an automated test suite on every change.

Skaters under 18

Figure skating is largely a junior sport and we expect many users to be minors. A younger skater’s account collects nothing beyond what an adult’s does, and is subject to exactly the same access rules.

Parents and guardians. You may ask to see, correct or delete a child’s account at any time by emailing us, and we will act on it without requiring the child’s agreement. If you would rather the account simply not exist, say so and it will be deleted outright.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it. There is no automatic expiry, because a training log is only useful across seasons. Deleting your account removes everything described above.

Deleting your account

In the app: open the account sheet from the header and choose Delete account. You confirm by typing your username, and the deletion is immediate and total — the sign-in goes too, not just the data behind it.

You can also request deletion from this website without installing anything.

Your other rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask what we hold. Email us and we will answer. Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) you may also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada if you are unhappy with how we have handled a request.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Access rules live in the database rather than the application, which is the meaningful difference: a bug in the app cannot hand out data the database will not release. Passwords are stored and verified by Supabase’s authentication service and are never visible to us.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who can see it, the app will tell you rather than quietly updating this page. The effective date at the top always reflects the current version.
